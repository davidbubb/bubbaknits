Before you order

Terms and conditions

A few useful details about prices, privacy, returns and delivery.

Prices

Prices shown are for knitted items only. Teddy bears, toys, hearts and butterflies are not included unless stated otherwise.

Privacy

Customer information is used to process and fulfil your order. It is not sold or shared with third parties, except when necessary to arrange delivery.

Returns

I take pride in the quality of my work. If you are not happy with an item, you may return it within 14 days of receiving it for a no-quibble refund, provided it is unworn and returned in satisfactory condition. Postage and packing charges are not included in this guarantee.

Please get in touch before returning an item. This guarantee does not affect your statutory rights.

Delivery

In-stock items are posted the following day. Delivery times below are estimates and depend on the selected postage service.

Postage and packing

  • Orders up to £5.00: £3 postage and packing, 1st class next-day delivery.
  • Orders from £5.01 to £14.99: £6, parcel post (3–5 working days).
  • Orders from £15.00 to £49.99: £9, parcel post (3–5 working days).
  • Orders of £50.00 or more: free postage.
