About Bubba Knits

Welcome and thanks for looking at my website. I’m an independent crafter based in Ash, near Canterbury in Kent.

My website shows a selection of my hand knitted items for babies and toddlers which are all available in different colours and sizes upon request.

Please feel free to browse and contact me if you have any requests or questions.

Made by hand

Every item is traditionally hand-knitted by me. The collection includes clothing and accessories for newborns, babies and toddlers.

Made to order

Many designs can be made in different colours and sizes. If you have a particular request, get in touch and I’ll be happy to discuss it.
